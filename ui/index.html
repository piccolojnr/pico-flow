Flow Linux

Private voice dictation for your desktop
Ready · hold Ctrl+Super to dictate

Hold Ctrl + Super while speaking. Release either key to transcribe and paste into the window you were using. Press Ctrl + Super + Space twice to start and stop hands-free listening.

Transcription

Audio is captured from your system default microphone through PipeWire and sent to Groq only when you release the shortcut. Audio is held in memory and a private temporary WAV file, then deleted after the request.

Behavior

History stores text only and keeps up to 500 entries. Existing history is preserved.
